01 / Construct

Turn documentation into supervision.

Four empirical sources and one literature-derived source supply the evidence. Questions progress from direct comprehension, through multi-attribute reasoning, to cross-dimensional interpretation.

The focus is interpreting supplied descriptions, rather than computing directly over raw trajectories.

02 / Teach

Ask, challenge, rephrase.

Generate an answer. Verify whether a candidate is supported. Paraphrase while retaining required structure and answers.

Incorrect candidates belong in verification inputs, not generation targets. Related concepts and their descendants stay in the same split.

03 / Test

Change the source, check the claim.

Five backbones × two checkpoints × three settings produce 30 evaluation combinations. The broader external benchmark reveals weaknesses that the regional test can conceal.

Try fine-tuned Llama 70B: verification falls from 99.38% on France/Japan to 92.71% on the ten-dataset benchmark.

The finding to remember

Fine-tuning improves structured output compliance, short-answer reference overlap and automated paraphrase preservation. Correctness gains vary by task and model. The 8B Llama leads in-domain verification; the 70B leads the external multiple-choice and verification aggregates.

Fluent answers, correct answers and reference-like answers are different things. Both Llama models already have 100% normalized in-domain multiple-choice accuracy before tuning. Low similarity can also reflect valid wording or mismatched scoring targets.

Limits: only ten held-out in-domain concept groups; one training run per model; uneven source coverage by reasoning level; a different prequantized 70B base; and a small, provisional qualitative audit. These experiments do not establish general planning competence or a causal model-size advantage. T5 base verification scores of 0% reflect unparseable verdicts.
